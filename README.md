# Rush Hour Kitchen

A complete original 2D cooking game for the make.fun "Make the best cooking game" bounty. Played with keyboard and mouse in a desktop browser, from static files under `game/` with no build step and no network requests - every script, image and sound is home-grown (canvas-drawn art, Web Audio synth beeps).

## The game

Rush hour is here and tickets keep flying. Watch the order board, pick ingredients from the counter (keys 1-6 or click), stack the dish on the plate and serve each ticket before its countdown dies. Exact matches score points; wrong or late tickets cost time and score. Pressure grows as the shift goes on. When the shift timer hits zero, it is game over and the final score shows.

## Controls

- 1-6: pick that ingredient from the counter
- click a bin: pick that ingredient
- Enter or Space: serve the plate
- Backspace: clear the plate
- Space or click: start (title screen) / play again (game over)

Serve locally: `node serve.ts`, then open http://localhost:8080. Or open `game/index.html` directly.
